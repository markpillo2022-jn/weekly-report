How these numbers work
  • Each client is one Meta ad account. All of them are managed by Mark Pilio.
  • Every campaign that spent money in the last 30 days is listed under its client. Active campaigns come first (green dot), then paused ones.
  • ROAS is Meta's purchase conversion value divided by spend for the same period, using each campaign's own attribution setting. Each client has its own target, shown in its header and at the top. Green meets it, red misses it. "No sales" means money was spent but no purchase value was recorded. A dash means nothing was spent.
  • Last 7 and last 30 days end yesterday in each account's time zone. Overall is the whole life of the campaign (or the account, on the client header) and includes today so far.
  • The blended ROAS at the top has no single target, so it isn't colored.
  • Worksport and Swift are billed in Canadian dollars, the others in US dollars. Each client shows its own currency. The blended ROAS at the top adds Worksport's CAD to the USD clients without converting them, so treat it as a rough guide.
  • Swift runs lead campaigns, so its box shows cost per lead (spend divided by Meta's Leads count) instead of ROAS, and it is left out of the totals at the top. "No leads" means money was spent but no leads were recorded.
