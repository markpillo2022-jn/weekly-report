How these numbers work
  • Clients are the ones marked Active with Facebook Ads Status Green in the ClickUp Client Directory, grouped by their Facebook Ads Specialist.
  • Only campaigns whose name starts with "JN Conversion" count. Accounts with no such campaign are left out.
  • Cost per lead is spend divided by Meta's Leads count for the same period. Green is at or under the target, red is over it. "No leads" means money was spent but no leads were recorded. A dash means nothing was spent.
  • N/A means Meta doesn't let the report read that ad account yet. Amber rows were typed in by the team from Ads Manager.
  • Mark Pilio Onboarding lists the clients on Mark's ClickUp onboarding tasks (Onboarding › OB - Facebook Ads) that are not live yet. Stage is the most advanced ClickUp status across that client's tasks. Last activity is the latest task update or comment, red after 7 days. Summary is the latest ClickUp comment. Overall status is typed in by Mark on the team dashboard and copied here at each update.
